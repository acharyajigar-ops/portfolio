Veeam backup overhaul

Backup & DR · 320 VMs · 140 TB
Challenge
Backup Exec jobs failing nightly, no offsite copy, no immutability, and a ransomware near-miss.
Approach
Veeam v12 on a scale-out repository, S3 Object Lock immutable tier, WAN-accelerated offsite copy, SureBackup lab to prove restores.
Results
  • Restore time cut from 4 h to 22 min
  • 320 VMs / 140 TB under full 3-2-1-1 coverage
  • 100% success across 8 quarterly DR tests
  • $95K storage savings from deduplication
